📂 Backup Drive

A firm keeps a backup drive of every case document in one folder structure everybody knows by heart: a Case Manager covering someone else's file still finds the executed retainer, the demand package and the lien correspondence where they always are. Practise it on your own Google Drive: connect it once, lay the firm's structure out yourself, and it is graded automatically, folder by folder. A layout that is right is then made in your Drive, and you keep it there.
