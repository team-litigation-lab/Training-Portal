🧭 Control Directory
Click Open to go there, or ⓘ for step-by-step instructions. Every panel has the same ⓘ How to use button at its top.

Log-in approvals: every registration waiting, 🧑‍🏫 trainers on their own, then trainees by the batch they will join (oldest registration first in each), whether someone signed up on the login screen or a trainer set the account up here. Approve moves the account into the Users tab and issues its Batch ID.

All approved logins: 🧑‍🏫 trainers on their own, then one table per batch (newest first; click a batch to open or close it). Edit changes a login's Batch ID, name, email, username or password; Suspend, Restore and Revoke (the whole account) are on each row. For one program at a time, use Program Access.

Revoke takes a trainee out of every training program (Foundational, EA / PA, Case Management, PD Claims, Medsum & Demand, Lien Negotiator), so they leave every Batch Sheet, ranking and scorecard there. This button does the same for anyone revoked before that: their records are filed to their batch's folder (or downloaded), then deleted.
🟢 Users Online

Who is signed in to this page: 🧑‍🏫 trainers on their own, then one table per batch (newest first; click a batch to open or close it). Each row shows what they are viewing and when their page last checked in. Away means no check-in for over 90 seconds: the tab was closed or went to sleep without logging out. ↻ Refresh reloads the list.
