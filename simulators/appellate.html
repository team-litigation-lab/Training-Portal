⚖️ Appellate E-Filing

Appeals are filed by their own rules, and the rules are where appeals are lost. Take a case up: count the deadline to appeal and file the notice of appeal in the trial court with the record designated, file the opening brief in the court of appeals inside the word limit, behind the right cover, with the excerpts of record and the certificates the court requires, and get an emergency stay in front of a judge before the sheriff levies on Monday. Pick the state you file in and the deadlines, fees, word limits and cover colors change with it.
