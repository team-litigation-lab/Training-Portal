📚 Demand Packets & Binders

Put a full packet together the way the firm sends it: the cover letter, the index, the demand letter, the PIP log, the medical bills and the medical records, in order, as one PDF. Add as many PDFs (or scans and photos) as each section needs; the page numbers them, writes the index with each document's page range, stamps the Bates numbers and bookmarks every section.
