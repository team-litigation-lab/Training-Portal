What's inside

Built around how trainees actually learn.

No scattered spreadsheets or email threads — activities, lectures, grades, and announcements all live in the same place, for every batch.

📋

Structured Daily Activities

Short, day-by-day modules with objective questions that grade themselves instantly, plus admin review for anything that needs a human eye.

🎬

Recorded Lectures, On Demand

Watch training sessions embedded straight from Drive or YouTube in a small pop-out player — pause, rewatch, or expand to full view, any time.

📈

Real-Time Progress & Leaderboard

Trainees always know exactly where they stand. Admins see completion and grading status across every batch, live.

📣

Announcements, Alerts & Pings

Reach one trainee or the whole portal instantly — a scrolling ticker for updates, full-screen alerts for anything urgent.

How it works

From application to graduation.

A straightforward path whether you're just applying or already deep into training.

1

Register & Get Approved

Submit your registration; an admin reviews it and assigns you a Batch ID to get started.

2

Complete Daily Activities

Work through structured, day-by-day modules — most grade themselves the moment you submit.

3

Watch Recorded Lectures

Catch up on training sessions on your own schedule, any time you need a refresher.

4

Track Progress & Graduate

Watch your score climb on the leaderboard as you complete your batch's training program.

Organized by batch

Every trainee has a home — their Batch ID.

Registration, grading, monitoring, and leaderboards are all organized around the batch a trainee belongs to, so admins can see how a whole cohort is progressing at a glance — not just one person at a time.
