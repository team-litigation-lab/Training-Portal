⚖️ Docket System

Work like a law-firm docketing clerk. Court notices and mail land in your inbox: put what belongs on the court docket there, and calendar every deadline it triggers, counted under the court's rules, with a responsible attorney and reminders. Then check your docket against the key. Court Records keeps a federal-style docket report for every case; the Deadline Calculator does the counting once you've learned it.
