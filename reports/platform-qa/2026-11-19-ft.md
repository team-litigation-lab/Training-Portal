The one thing to fix first: **this program lets in anyone who holds any Portal ticket** (finding 1). The Worker checks
only the ticket's signature and its expiry, and then marks the trainee approved. A ticket the Portal made for a tool or
for another program therefore signs a trainee into Standard Foundational Training even when the Portal never approved
them for it. The same ticket also works again and again for its 5 minutes.

**Note on the Portal side.** While this audit ran, another session on this branch committed `8ee4167` ("SSO tickets name the program or tool they are for (p) and carry the full registered name"). It changes `functions/api/launch.js`, `sso-check.js`, `ticket-login.js` and `verify-ticket.js`, and its `makeTicket` adds `p` (the program's topic key or the tool id) and `full` to trainee tickets. That commit is not this audit's work and is not counted above. I checked the new ticket shape against this Worker. **`full` works here as soon as it is deployed**, because FT already reads it. **`p` closes nothing yet**: FT ignores it. With a ticket carrying `p: "kb"` or `p: "LIEN NEGOTIATION"`, FT still answered 200 and signed the trainee in. Patch 1a below is the FT half.

## Findings

### 1. Any Portal ticket opens this program and makes the holder an approved trainee  — high
Where: `worker.js:248-266` (`readPortalTicket`), `:3135-3182` (`/api/auth/portal`). Portal: `functions/api/launch.js:202`
(tools skip the access check), `:210` (the trainee ticket)   Category: Disconnect (SSO)

What happens (plain): FT trusts every pass the Portal signs. It never asks whether the pass was made for FT.
1. A trainee is approved on the Portal and has a batch, but has no access to Standard Training (never requested,
   Pending, or another program only).
2. They open a Portal tool, such as `/api/launch?tool=kb`. Tools skip the access check (`launch.js:202`). The Portal
   redirects them with `?ticket=…`.
3. They paste that ticket into `https://foundational-training…workers.dev/?ticket=…` within 5 minutes.
4. FT signs them in and writes `ft:trainee:<name--batch>` with `approved: true` (`worker.js:3172-3180`).

Verified with a probe that calls `worker.fetch` with a ticket built exactly as `makeTicket` builds it:
`200`, record `approved: true`. The same ticket again gave `200` (it is not single-use). With the Portal's new ticket
shape (`p: "kb"`, `p: "LIEN NEGOTIATION"`), the result was also `200`.

Why the Portal's block list doesn't catch it: `programBlock` (`_ft-block.js`) blocks only records that **already exist**
in `ft:`. A trainee who never had an FT record has nothing to block, so their first sign-in creates an approved record.

Fix: proposed (auth, and a cross-repo contract). It goes with the Portal's `p` change (`8ee4167`).

Patch 1a: FT `worker.js`. The ticket must be for this program, and a trainee ticket works once.

### 3. A Calendar Management Mock Call scored on the Portal never reaches FT after a Batch ID change or an account merge  — medium
Where: `worker.js:3208-3211` (`/api/calsim-card-in` looks up only `newId` and `rawId`); `worker.js:278-299` (`movedBatch`
keeps the old id); Portal `functions/api/calsim.js:52-63,135` (the result is returned as `mirrored` and no page reads it)   Category: Bug

1. Mark Lee registered in batch B100126. His FT record is `mark-lee--b100126`.
2. The Portal moves him to B110226. On his next sign-in FT keeps his record under the **old** id and sets its `batch` to
   B110226 (`movedBatch`). That is by design, so his progress stays.
3. A trainer scores his Calendar Management Mock Call on the Portal's Google Calendar Simulator. The Portal sends
   `{name: "Mark Lee", batch: "B110226"}` to `/api/calsim-card-in`.
4. FT looks for `mark-lee--b110226` only. That record is missing, or archived as a merged duplicate. FT answers

### 4. A trainee blocked on the Portal can still sign in for up to a minute, and a refused sign-in still rewrites their record  — low
Where: `worker.js:3111` (`blockedIds(env)` with no `fresh`); `:3160-3165` (`movedBatch` and `withFull` write before the
block check)   Category: Disconnect (COURSE-PROGRAMS.md contract, step b)

What happens: the contract says `traineeSession` reads the block list fresh: `blockedIds(env, true)`. FT reads it from
a per-instance cache that is up to 60 s old. Verified: block a trainee right after a sign-in, sign in again at once, and the
result is `200` with a new token. Sixty-one seconds later it is `403 access-offboarded`, and their existing token is refused
too. Separately, when a blocked trainee's Batch ID has changed, `movedBatch` moves the record to the new batch and archives
any newer duplicate. Only then does `:3165` refuse the sign-in. The refusal holds, but a blocked record is rewritten.
Fix: proposed (auth). Change `:3111` to `blockedIds(env, true)`. Pass the block list into `movedBatch` and return early,
without writing, when the old record's id is on it.

### 5. A middle name never appears on the certificate, even when FT has it  — low
Where: Portal `launch.js:210-211` (adds `full` since `8ee4167`, not yet deployed); FT `index.html:12022-12024` (`certData`)   Category: Disconnect

What happens: once the Portal sends `full`, FT keeps `fullName: "Maria L. Santos"` (verified with the new ticket shape).
The Trainee Audit and attendance show it. But the Certificate of Completion builds the name from `firstName` +
`lastName`, or from `certName`, and never from `fullName`. So the certificate still says "Maria Santos" unless the
trainee types a certificate name themselves.
Fix: proposed (it changes a document a trainee receives). Use `src.fullName` before `formatCertName(...)` when
`certName` is empty, and use `state.fullName` in the same way on the trainee side, from the record loaded at sign-in.

## Learning features (ranked)

### 1. Tap a legal term in a lesson to see what it means  — effort S (link) / M (in-slide)
**What:** words such as "subrogation", "PIP" or "letter of representation" in a lesson's slides are underlined. Tapping
one shows a one-line definition, with a link to the full glossary.
**Why it helps the trainee:** Day 2 has an assessment on exactly this, "Common Terms Used — Assessment"
(`js/ft-day-activities.js`, day 2). The platform already holds about 700 definitions (`glossary.html`, the `RAW` list) and the
program's own reference terms (`js/ft-ref-terms-data.js`, `FT_REF_TERMS`). Neither reaches the trainee (finding 6). They
meet the terms on Day 2 and keep using them through Day 16 with nothing to look them up in.
**Where:** a small `js/ft-terms.js` that loads `FT_REF_TERMS` (and the glossary's `RAW` list, moved into a `.js` file) and
marks terms in the deck pages that `js/ft-day-decks.js` renders. Start with the S version: a 📖 Glossary button in
`js/lsh-topbar.js`. It can share its list with the Portal's proposed simulator glossary (Portal feature 7).
**Dependencies:** none. The definitions already exist. The program owner should check FT_REF_TERMS against the lessons.

### 2. Re-answer your weakest Knowledge Check answers a few days later  — effort M
**What:** when a trainee opens a later lesson, a short card shows one or two process questions they scored 5/10 or less on
earlier, with the feedback they got. It asks them to write the answer again. This practice is not graded.
**Why it helps the trainee:** the Knowledge Check is "taken once" (`kc-once`; `js/ft-process.js:849-855`). Each answer gets a
score out of 10 and feedback, and then is never seen again. What a trainee missed on Lesson 2 (Law Firm Communication) is
still missed when they need it on the Day 7 intake calls.
**Where:** read from the grading record `js/ft-process.js` already keeps per answer (`{sid, i, score, feedback}`, `:1020-1025`).
Show it as a card on the lesson page or in 📚 Dashboard. Save the re-answer to the trainee's own `process:<id>` record. The
trainer can see it in ✍️ Process Questions.
**Dependencies:** no new keys, and no AI is needed (it shows the earlier feedback beside the new answer). The optional
AI re-grade could reuse the transcription activity's "cached, throttled and capped" pattern.

### 3. A practice question before the graded Knowledge Check  — effort M
**What:** before the one graded attempt, a trainee can answer one sample process question and get the same kind of
feedback, without it counting.
**Why it helps the trainee:** today the first written answer a trainee gives is the graded one. A trainee who doesn't yet
know what "complete sentences, accuracy, depth, clarity" (`js/ft-process.js:976`) looks like in practice loses points on the
format, not on what they know.
**Where:** `js/ft-process.js`, with the same grader prompt (`:1029-1049`) on one question flagged `practice: true` and saved
apart from the attempt. Or, at no AI cost, show one model answer that the trainer writes per lesson.
**Dependencies:** an AI call per practice answer (capped per trainee per lesson), or trainer-written model answers.

### 4. "What to practise next": your weakest scorecard metric across all mock calls  — effort M
**What:** on the trainee's progress page, one line such as "Across your Reception, Calendar and Intake mock calls,
*Information Verification & Accuracy* is your lowest (2.5/5). Practise it on the Call Simulator", with a link.
**Why it helps the trainee:** each 🧑‍🏫 card already shows that call's rubric scores and remarks (`js/ft-sessions.js:424-428`).
But they are spread over three cards on different days, so nobody adds them up. Several metrics appear on more than one
rubric (verification, notes and recap, call control), and a weakness that repeats is the one worth practising.
**Where:** computed from `labreview:<id>.acts[*].rubric` (the same records `/api/calsim-card-in` and Graders write).
The link goes to the Portal's `simulators/call.html?program=FT&line=…`, which the Practice Lab already uses
(`js/ft-sessions.js:55-56`). It needs finding 3 fixed, so that the Portal's calendar cards are counted.
**Dependencies:** none (no new keys, no AI).

### 5. Show trainees their own lesson progress on the Portal  — effort M (Portal)
The Portal audit's feature 1 ("My progress" on each program card) depends on finding 2 for FT. With `days: 10`, every FT
trainee would see themselves at 80% at best. Fix finding 2 first, then build it.

## Verified working
- **Single sign-on basics:** a bad signature gets 401 `signature`, an expired ticket gets 401, and a ticket with no
  batch is refused. An admin ticket works once (`sso.cjs` passes). The ticket is removed from the address bar before
  anything else runs (`js/portal-gate.js:24-31`).
- **`access:blocked`:** blocked trainees are dropped from every list (`listAll`, `js/ft-roster.js`). Sign-in is refused (after
  the cache window; finding 4), and an existing token is refused on every request (verified: 403 `access-offboarded`).
  The key is `ft:access:blocked`, the same as the Portal's `_ft-block.js` writes.
- **Keys the Portal reads:** `ft:trainee:<name--batch>` with `dayProgress`, `practiceProgress`, `roleplayHistory`, `approved`,
  `archived` and `rejected` (written by `syncToLedger`, `index.html:5661-5684`). Also `ft:feedback:<id>`, `ft:tfeedback:*`,
  `ft:checkin:<date>:<id>` (with list metadata `{t, at, n, b, tr}`), and `ft:attendance:<batch key>:<date>` (`setTimeIn`
  changes only the timeIn of that one row). All match `program-progress.js` and `_attendance.js`.
- **IDs:** `candidateIds` (`worker.js:365-370`) matches the Portal's `courseIdsFor` forms, which are first + last name and
  the batch in canonical and as-typed form.
- **Every inline handler:** 204 distinct function names in `onclick` / `onchange` / … across `index.html`, `js/*.js` and
  the standalone pages. Each one is defined.
- **Every `/api/*` path the pages call:** 51 of them, and each has a route in `worker.js`.
- **Links to the Portal:** `attendance.html`, `programs.html`, `blueprint`, `simulators/{call,calendar,drafting,email,records}.html`
  and `api/launch?tool=kb` all exist in the Portal repo. Links to the KB, CMS and Ring Channel are proxy-blocked here and
  covered by the Portal's Uptime run.
- **Browser walk** (Playwright, an approved trainee, 1360 and 390 px): 📚 Dashboard, Lesson 1, Lesson 5, 🛠 Practice Lab
  and 🧪 Typing & Spelling. No horizontal scroll at either width, no page errors, and every visible Dashboard button was
  clicked (40 at desktop, 43 at phone). The one 400 (`/api/drive/verify` with no id) is an artefact of open mode: the
  test server has no admin password, so every caller counts as an admin.
- **Rubrics are shown before and after** each trainer-led mock call (`js/ft-sessions.js:424-428`). Knowledge Check
  answers get feedback per answer (`js/ft-process.js:678,991`). KC pass marks are 70% everywhere (`js/ft-process.js:855`,
  README).
- **Public files:** `.assetsignore` keeps `worker.js`, the answer-key modules (`assess-keys.js`, `transcribe-keys.js`),
  `*.md`, `build/` and `.github/` off the site. `/trainer/*` goes through the Worker and needs an admin token.
  `docs/eod-gmail-script.gs` is public, but its key is a script property and is not in the file.
