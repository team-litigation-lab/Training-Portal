About Legal Support Help

Meet our Founder

CEO Sandy Van, Esq.

Attorney Sandy Van is the CEO of Legal Support Help, a virtual staffing agency that sources, trains, and onboards high-performing Virtual Assistants for law firms and medical facilities nationwide.

She is also the founder and managing partner of Van Law Firm Injury and Accident Attorneys, where she leads a team specializing in personal injury, mass tort, and sexual abuse litigation.

With over 15 years of legal experience and more than a decade in real estate, Attorney Van combines deep industry expertise with operational excellence. She has managed hundreds of multi-skilled Virtual Assistants, featured in Inc.com, and frequently speaks at industry conventions, events, and podcasts.

Our History

Legal Support Help (LSH) was established to assist professionals (e.g. Attorneys) and businesses (e.g. Law Firms, Medical Facilities) in delegating highly administrative, routine, and time-consuming tasks to reclaim their time and be more productive and efficient in both their professional and personal priorities. Founded in 2018.

Officially registered in Las Vegas, Nevada. 100% remote. Setting standards in legal and medical VA outsourcing. Providing law firm standard training to qualified virtual assistants. Making outsourcing processes faster for potential clients. Providing better virtual assistant services at affordable prices to clients.

Mission, Vision and Core Values

CLIENTS

Mission

To empower law firms, medical facilities, and businesses to grow and thrive by providing pre-trained, multi-skilled virtual assistants who deliver reliable, efficient, and cost-effective virtual support, with fast onboarding and flexible staffing solutions.

Vision

To be the leading and most trusted provider of pre-trained, multi-skilled virtual assistants, empowering law firms, medical facilities, and businesses to grow and thrive. By setting the standard for reliable, efficient, and cost-effective virtual support without compromising quality, we help our clients achieve sustainable, long-term success.

VA

Mission

To empower aspiring virtual assistants by equipping them with foundational knowledge and essential skills, enabling them to become multi-skilled virtual assistants who are competitive and can thrive in both legal and business environments.

Vision

To become the leading and most trusted staffing agency for aspiring virtual assistants by providing and delivering industry-standard quality training, structured onboarding, and reliable operational support. By providing a strong operational backbone, we empower virtual assistants to grow their careers and enhance their quality of life.
